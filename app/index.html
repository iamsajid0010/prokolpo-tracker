Ashulia Union projects (synthetic sample)

This page lets you drill down Division, District, Upazila and Union, open a project, read its price audit against benchmark rates, its daily log and a next-day statistical projection, and ask a rule-based retrieval copilot. JavaScript is needed for the selectors; the table below is the pre-rendered seed summary.

Seed dates are synthetic and shifted to the current date: daily records and logs are stored as day offsets and resolved when the page loads, so the series always ends yesterday. Union list is a demo list; verify with official LGD data. Three unions (Ashulia, Birulia, Barabkunda) have hand-authored projects; all others honestly show "no data yet".
