Demo User

One click opens a ready citizen session: persona Demo Citizen in Ashulia Union, Savar, Dhaka. No form and no login. JavaScript renders nearby alerts, your union projects and the anonymous report form.

Anonymous reports get a locally generated random ID (crypto.randomUUID) and the status "Saved locally in demo; not transmitted". Nothing is sent to any server.
