Pitch Deck

A 10-slide deck. With JavaScript it becomes a one-slide viewer with arrow keys and fullscreen; without it, all slides are stacked below. Printing gives one slide per page. Market figures are estimates to be validated; traction is a working prototype with no users yet.

1 / 10

Problem

Public project information is hard to find and harder to compare. Citizens, journalists and local officials lack one simple view.

2 / 10

Solution

Prokolpo Tracker: Union-level project pages with price checks, disbursement checks, a transparent projection and a bilingual copilot.

3 / 10

Market (to be validated)

Illustrative framing: Bangladesh has several thousand Union Parishads and a large annual development budget. Sizing must be validated with official sources before any claim.

4 / 10

Product

Working prototype: 8 divisions, 3 fully seeded unions, 10 synthetic projects, price audit, projection, copilot, anonymous local reports, admin demo, real in-browser audit.

5 / 10

Traction

Honest status: working prototype, no users yet, no real data.

6 / 10

Business model (illustrative)

Ideas to test: grants for transparency tools, paid support for local governments, and open data services. None validated.

7 / 10

Growth

Next: pilot with one Upazila using real public data, then publish methods and results openly.

8 / 10

Team

Solo builder (individual competition). Advisors: Planned.

9 / 10

Impact (targets)

Targets only: easier citizen review, earlier detection of disbursement gaps. No results achieved yet.
