Presentation

A five-section product story with a working 3:00 timer (needs JavaScript). All five sections are shown in full below. KPIs are targets, not achieved results.

1. Problem

Citizens cannot easily see what a local public project costs, how far it has progressed, or whether prices look reasonable.

2. Solution

A free static web tool: drill down to a Union, compare billed prices with benchmark rates, check disbursement against progress, and anonymously flag logs.

3. Walkthrough

Open /app (Ashulia is preselected) → open a project → Price audit tab → Overview for the projection formula → ask the copilot → try /demo-admin and /audit.

4. AI approach

Transparent and simple: a least-squares projection, a rule engine and keyword retrieval. Every output shows its inputs and sources. No LLM is used yet. Humans decide.

5. Impact

Targets, not achieved results: faster citizen review of project data, earlier visibility of disbursement gaps, and a reusable open pattern for transparency tools.
